Logos

For my logo, I wanted something fun and something that looked like it has movement. I didn't want anything sharp, angular or heavy. The handwritten script felt the right amount of imperfect! The diamond comes from my name: "Syamantaka" which is a jewel that belongs to the Hindu God Krishna. I chose a warm colour palette because it matches my personality. I wanted the colours to look soft. I didn't want it to look futuristic and corporate.
