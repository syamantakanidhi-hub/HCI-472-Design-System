Hey, I'm Sam Nidhi

I am a human-centered UX Designer and Researcher who translates complex human friction into soft, intentional digital experiences. Moving into design from a background in architecture, I view generative AI as a collaborative thinking partner and design systems as living blueprints for empathy.

My design aesthetic avoids rigid, corporate future-facing trends. Instead, I practice a philosophy centered around playful, fluid storytelling, warmth, and imperfect, organic line-work. Whether I am mapping a user's emotional triggers, building rapid visual prototypes, or crafting tangible physical art, my goal is always the same: to create soft, accessible interfaces that feel deeply human.
